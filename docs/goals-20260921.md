# High-level Goals and Initial Setup

From session transcript of discussion on 2026-09-21.

## Flexibility Requirements

- Non-standard experiments are common and require script modifications
- Bluesky at NSLS-II was difficult to adapt for non-standard experiments
- Need a platform with flexibility to handle custom experiment configurations

## AI-Driven Experiment Control

- AI tools need good building blocks to construct objective plans
- Two operational approaches:
  1. **Synthesis-based**: Let experiment run, then AI redirects based on results
  2. **Real-time adaptive**: AI modifies parameters on-the-fly based on live data analysis
- Variable parameters: time, temperature, composition, gases

## Sample Materials & Considerations

- Primary focus: inorganic materials (battery cathodes, new material synthesis)
- Q: Is beam damage of the sample a concern?
- A: Material stability varies: some can remain in beam indefinitely, others decompose or sustain damage
